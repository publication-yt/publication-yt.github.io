Publication

A private tool used by one independent creator to schedule uploads of their own videos to their own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files from the creator's computer to their YouTube channels.
  • Sets the title, description, tags and scheduled publication date of each video.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API v3 with the youtube.upload permission only.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account.
